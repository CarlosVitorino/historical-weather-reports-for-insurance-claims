# Existing US spend on historical weather verification / weather-evidence products (2024–2026)

Method note: these notes come from about 20 web searches done in October 2026. A direct fetch of verisk.com failed (DNS), so Verisk facts come from search-result summaries of Verisk pages. Most company revenue figures are third-party aggregator estimates (ZoomInfo, Owler, Datanyze, Seamless.ai), not disclosures. None of these companies publishes audited revenue for weather-verification products. Verisk is public but does not break out Benchmark. Confidence labels: **High** = primary source with a figure; **Med** = credible secondary source or a consistent aggregator; **Low** = my estimate or a single aggregator.

Price points are already collected in `docs/research_notes/Competitor SEO and ads positioning/competitors.md` and `enterprise_audiences.md`. They are reused below only as inputs to the volume math.

## 1. Revenue, headcount and customer signals for existing players

### Takeaway
Every independent player in this space is small: under $5M revenue and under about 100 staff each, on aggregator estimates. The only large companies (Verisk, Cotality) bundle weather verification inside claims platforms and do not report it separately. No company publishes a count of reports sold. The single volume datapoint is Verisk's "hundreds of thousands" of Benchmark reports over about 2 years, from around 2013–2015.

### Cited Findings
**Contractor hail-map / storm-lead tools**
- HailTrace (Edmond, OK). ZoomInfo puts revenue under $5M and headcount at 11–50, and claims "over 10,000 satisfied clients". The client claim is unverified. — [ZoomInfo](https://zoominfo.com/c/hail-trace/356109358)
- HailTrace revenue is $1M–$5M with 51–200 employees according to Seamless.ai. Prospeo gives about 24 employees. — [Seamless.ai](https://seamless.ai/b/hailtrace-115215235); [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/c/hailtrace-email-format)
- HailTrace had 52 employees, remote across 6 states, per an HR-vendor (CoAdvantage) case study, undated. This is the best headcount datapoint. — [CoAdvantage case study PDF](https://coadvantage.com/hubfs/Case%20Studies/Complete_with_Docusign_HailTrace_Case_Study_.pdf?hsLang=en)
- HailStrike (Plano, TX; founded 2011; a Dynamic Weather Solutions company). Owler estimates revenue at $100K–$5.0M with 25–100 employees. — [Owler](https://www.owler.com/company/hailstrike); [Craft](https://craft.co/hailstrike); [Dallas Innovates](https://dallasinnovates.com/plano-entrepreneur-makes-hail-hot-commodity/)
- HailStrike is distributed through CertainTeed's credentialed-contractor program, which shows manufacturer-channel distribution. — [Roofing Contractor](https://roofingcontractor.com/articles/90724-certainteed-partners-with-hailstrike-to-connect-credentialed-contractors-with-storm-torn-roofs)
- Interactive Hail Maps / Hail Recon: no revenue or subscriber figure was found. The Hail Recon iOS companion app has about 85 reviews at 3.0★, and AppGoblin estimates about 235 monthly active users (low reliability). The app is free and needs an IHM subscription ($999–$1,999/yr per competitors.md). — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/602877045); [AppFollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/hail-recon/602877045?country=us)
- A roofing trade article names HailWatch, HailTrace and Hail Recon as the leading hail apps for roofers. — [Roofing Magazine](https://roofingmagazine.com/mobile-app-provides-roofing-contractors-with-hail-storm-data-at-exact-locations/)

**Weather data / report sellers**
- Weather Source (Salem, NH; founded 2004): about $3.4M revenue and about 15 employees per Datanyze. — [Datanyze](https://www.datanyze.com/companies/weather-source/109969603)
- AnythingWeather (Palm Desert, CA; since 1995) claims an archive built from 20,000+ sources and delivery usually within 24–48h. It gives no client or report counts. — [AnythingWeather About](https://www.anythingweather.com/resources/about-anythingweather/)
- CompuWeather (established 1976) is a forensic consulting firm that does past-weather reports and expert testimony for insurance professionals. It gives no volume or revenue figures. — [PC360 sponsor listing](https://www.event.propertycasualty360.com/ACE-19/sponsor/17764/compuweather)
- StormRecord, WeatherGuidance, StormerSite: no revenue, headcount or volume data was found in any source.

**Carrier enterprise tools**
- Verisk FY2024 consolidated revenue was $2,882M (+7.5%). Q4 2024 Claims-segment growth of 13.0% was "mainly driven by property estimating solutions and anti-fraud solutions". Benchmark sits inside property estimating (Xactimate/XactAnalysis) but is not broken out. — [Verisk FY2024 results](https://www.verisk.com/company/newsroom/verisk-reports-fourth-quarter-2024-and-full-year-2024-financial-results/)
- Benchmark (hail, wind, hurricane, lightning) integrates with Xactimate, XactAnalysis and Guidewire. — [Verisk Benchmark](https://www.verisk.com/insurance/products/benchmark/); [Verisk/Guidewire release](https://www.verisk.com/company/newsroom/verisk-introduces-benchmark-reports-via-guidewire-to-streamline-weather-related-claims)
- ISO ClaimSearch has offered weather forensic reports (hail, lightning, wind, tornado), with AER as partner, through Decision Net "for a nominal fee" since 2010. — [Verisk/ISO + AER release](https://www.verisk.com/company/newsroom/iso-and-aer-team-up-to-deliver-weather-forensic-reports-through-iso-claimsearch)
- Cotality (formerly CoreLogic) sells Weather Verify, bundling hail, wind and lightning reports. It is marketed to insurers, contractors, engineers and attorneys. Earlier names were Weather Verification Service, HVT 3.0 and Weather Fusion. No volume or price is published. — [Cotality Weather Verify](https://cotality.com/products/weather-verify); [GPS World on CoreLogic weather forensics](https://www.gpsworld.com/corelogic-expands-insurance-offerings-with-hail-wind-and-lightning-weather-forensics/)
- Understory raised a $15M Series A in June 2024 (True Ventures, Prelude). It is now a full-stack MGA (auto-dealer hail cover across about 1,000 locations, plus solar) rather than a weather-verification vendor. Treat it as a non-competitor for this tier. — [The Insurer](https://theinsurer.com/news/understory-closes-15mn-series-a-led-by-true-ventures-and-prelude-ventures); [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/understory-insurance-platform-protecting-climate-vulnerable-industries-raises-15-million)

### Inferences
- The independent contractor/report vendors (HailTrace, HailStrike, IHM, Weather Source, AnythingWeather, CompuWeather) are each probably in the $1–5M range. Combined, the independents probably total **roughly $15–40M/yr US revenue** (Low confidence). Not all of that is "historical verification": HailTrace, HailStrike and IHM revenue is largely storm-lead generation for roofers.
- No company states that a meaningful share of its revenue comes from consumers or homeowners. The $9.99 single-report tier is the least-documented part of the market.

### Gaps
- No revenue, headcount or report count for StormRecord, WeatherGuidance, StormerSite or Interactive Hail Maps. Owler, Growjo and Crunchbase pages did not surface for them.
- No Benchmark or Cotality Weather Verify revenue or unit volume. The Verisk 10-K does not break them out.
- HailTrace's "10,000 clients" is unverified and probably counts free accounts.

## 2. How many weather verification reports carriers order per year

### Takeaway
No current annual figure is published. The only hard datapoint is Verisk's "hundreds of thousands" of Benchmark reports in about 2 years after integration into XactAnalysis (around 2013–2015). With roughly 2–3M wind/hail homeowners claims a year, carrier report volume is plausibly in the low millions per year today, but this is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- A 2015 Verisk release quotes a user saying that "hundreds of thousands of reports have been issued" in the two years since Benchmark was integrated into XactAnalysis. — [Verisk/Xactware release](https://verisk.com/company/newsroom/xactware-and-verisk-climate-make-it-easier-to-validate-weather-related-claims)
- Verisk research says about 30% of hail claims carry an incorrect date of loss. Carriers use this to justify routine date-of-loss verification. — same Verisk release, as summarized in search results
- Verisk ClaimSearch reports hail claim volume up 8% YoY in H1 2024, with 2023 the five-year high. Homeowners policies made up more than 68% of hail claims and personal auto about 27%. — [Verisk ClaimSearch Trends H1 2024](https://www.verisk.com/4aba4b/siteassets/media/campaigns/gated/claims/claimsearch-trends-report-h1-2024.pdf)
- Hail claims rose more than 40% in 2023 vs 2022, per Verisk Weather Solutions. — [The MortgagePoint](https://themortgagepoint.com/2024/11/19/hail-related-storm-damage-claims-on-the-rise/)
- NICB counted 637,977 Texas hail loss claims over 2017–2019. — [NICB](https://nicb.org/media/1923/download)
- III data (via Experian) shows wind/hail at 45.5% of homeowners claims in 2020, or about 1 in 35 insured homes, with an average of $11,695. In 2021 it was about 40% and $12,913. — [Experian](https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/most-common-homeowners-insurance-claims); [AssuredPartners](https://assuredpartners.com/news-insights/blogs/risk-management/2024/protect-your-home-against-wind-and-hail-damage)
- LexisNexis 2025 Home Trends (covering 2024): hail loss cost was 19% above the 7-year average, and overall claim frequency fell 11.6% from 2023 to 2024. — [LexisNexis via Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/35641779/lexisnexis-u-s-home-insurance-trends-report-highlights-rising-peril-severity-as-catastrophic-claims-hit-seven-year-high)
- State Farm paid $6.1B in hail claims in 2023, about $3.5B in 2022 and $5.6B in 2025. Its average homeowner hail claim was about $17,000. The NYT-quoted claim count of "27,300" is **internally inconsistent**: $6.1B ÷ 27,300 = about $223K per claim. The true count is likely about 273,000, but treat it as unverified. — [Rethinking65 summarizing NYT](https://rethinking65.com/insurers-report-rising-hail-damage-claims/); [Claims Journal 2026](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/04/23/337102.htm)

### Inferences
- **US wind/hail homeowners claims are about 2–3M/yr** (Low–Med). This is my arithmetic: III's "1 in 35 insured homes" applied to roughly 85–95M insured homes. It is consistent with State Farm alone paying hail claims at about $6B / $17K ≈ 350K claims.
- If carriers order an automated report (Benchmark, Cotality, ClaimSearch) on, say, 30–70% of wind/hail claims, that is **about 0.7–2M carrier reports/yr** (Low). At an unpublished per-report price plausibly in the $5–$30 range, carrier automated verification spend would be **about $5–60M/yr** (Low). Enterprise subscriptions (flat-fee, bundled with Xactimate) may make per-report pricing moot.
- The volume of claims where *someone* (carrier, contractor, PA, homeowner) wants address-level weather evidence is in the millions per year. That is the denominator for a $9.99 product. The open question is what share of claims involves a non-carrier buyer willing to pay.

### Gaps
- No current Verisk Benchmark or Cotality annual report counts. Xactimate integration statistics (users, reports per claim) were not found.
- No published per-report carrier price for Benchmark or Cotality.

## 3. NCEI certified data request volumes and revenue

### Takeaway
NCEI does not publish how many certified (court-admissible) records it sells. It handles more than 2M data requests a year, mostly free. Certified orders start at about $180, so certified-record revenue is probably small (Low confidence: well under $5M/yr).

### Cited Findings
- NCEI centers "respond to over 2,000,000 requests for these data and products annually from over 70 countries, the vast majority of which are fulfilled at no fee", per a 2023 Federal Register notice on NOAA's fee schedule. — [Federal Register 2023-17803](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2023-17803.pdf)
- Certified data is billed in 40-page increments. 1–40 pages costs $168 + $12 shipping = $180, and next-day delivery adds $17. — [NCEI Certification](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/services/certification)
- The Midwestern Regional Climate Center charges a $25 notarized certification fee plus $50/hr service. It noted the processing fee would rise to $50 in January 2024. — [MRCC pricing](https://www.mrcc.purdue.edu/data_serv/customOrdering.jsp)
- The Department of Commerce certification exists to meet the authentication requirements for court evidence. — [NCEI Certification](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/services/certification)

### Inferences
- If even 5,000–20,000 certified orders a year were sold at about $180–$250, revenue would be about $1–5M/yr. That range is a guess bracket, not data (Low). The fee schedule is set to recover costs, which suggests the program is small.

### Gaps
- No published count of certified orders or certification revenue. A FOIA request or the NOAA fee-schedule docket would be the route.

## 4. Forensic meteorology market

### Takeaway
The supply is tiny: about 240–260 active Certified Consulting Meteorologists, not all of them forensic. They bill about $300–$550/hr. This tier is probably tens of millions of dollars a year in the US, with engagements of about $1K–$25K+, and it serves litigation and disputed large claims, not routine claims.

### Cited Findings
- There were 242 active CCMs worldwide as of June 2020 and 248 as of February 2021, per AccuWeather. — [AccuWeather Forensics Overview](https://dev-business.accuweather.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2021/08/AccuWeather-Forensics-Overview.pdf); [AccuWeather](https://afb.accuweather.com/forensics-test)
- DTN says "only 258 meteorologists in the U.S." have been recognized as CCMs (undated, possibly cumulative). — [DTN](https://dtn.com/dtn-meteorologists-among-the-best-and-brightest-in-the-field)
- The AMS Weather & Climate Enterprise directory lists 143 CCM entries (directory listings, not the official count). — [AMS directory](https://wcdirectory.ametsoc.org/certified-consulting-meteorologists?page=9)
- Expert Institute profiles list forensic meteorologist rates of $305/hr review and $450/hr deposition/court (TX); $400 / $425 / $550 (CO); $390 / $490 (KS); $295 / $395 / $495 (NJ). — [Expert Institute e-471929](https://www.expertinstitute.com/expert/forensic-meteorology-expert-witness-e-471929/); [e-663975](https://www.expertinstitute.com/expert/forecasting-forensic-and-applied-meteorology-expert-witness-e-663975); [e-341641](https://www.expertinstitute.com/expert/meteorology-expert-witness-e-341641/); [NJ](https://www.expertinstitute.com/expert-witness/weather/new-jersey)
- HailTrace's productized "meteorologist certified" report costs $1,000, and its meteorologist-reviewed report costs $150. CompuWeather delivers signed CCM reports in about 5 business days at quote-only pricing. — see `enterprise_audiences.md` ([HailTrace reports](https://hailtrace.com/hail-reports); [CompuWeather](https://compuweather.com/))
- J.S. Held warns that automated services "repackage non-quality-checked data" and positions official storm reports and forensic analysis as the litigation standard. — [J.S. Held](https://www.jsheld.com/insights/articles/weather-reports-insurance-claims-finding-a-trustworthy-source)

### Inferences
- Rough supply-side ceiling: say 100–200 practitioners doing forensic weather work (CCMs plus non-certified meteorologists at firms like J.S. Held, AccuWeather, DTN and CompuWeather), each billing about 800–1,200 hrs/yr at about $350/hr. That gives **about $30–85M/yr** (Low). Not all of it is insurance work; some is slip-and-fall, transportation and construction.
- Case count is unknown. At about $3–10K per engagement, $30–85M implies **about 5,000–25,000 forensic engagements/yr** (Low).

### Gaps
- No current official AMS CCM count (2024–2026) was found. No count of forensic weather cases or engagements was found.

## 5. Market-research reports on weather verification / weather data for insurance

### Takeaway
No syndicated report sizes "weather verification for claims". The adjacent categories (parametric insurance, weather risk management, weather information tech) range from about $2B to $16B globally. They are irrelevant or heavily inflated as a proxy for this product and should not be used as TAM.

### Cited Findings
- Parametric insurance was $16.2B in 2024 and is projected at $51.3B by 2034 (12.6% CAGR). This is premium volume, not data spend. — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/05/01/3072182/0/en/Parametric-Insurance-Market-Assessment-2025-2034-Industry-Set-to-Reach-51-3-Billion-by-2034-as-Climate-Disasters-and-AI-Adoption-Reshape-Risk-Models.html)
- The "weather insurance market" was $2.18B in 2024 per Wise Guy Reports (premium, not data). — [Wise Guy Reports](https://www.wiseguyreports.com/reports/weather-insurance-market)
- "Weather risk management solutions" will be $12.7B in 2026 per MarkWide Research (methodology opaque). — [MarkWide](https://markwideresearch.com/weather-risk-management-solutions-market)
- "Weather information technologies" were about $1.9B globally in 2025 per Marketintelo, with North America the largest share. — [Marketintelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/weather-information-technologies-market)
- Fortune Business Insights has a weather forecasting services report (2026–2034) with a BFSI segment, covering forecasting rather than forensics. — [Fortune Business Insights](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/industry-reports/methodology/weather-forecasting-services-market-101365)

### Inferences
- These figures come from paid-report vendors using "secondary research + proprietary models", and their market definitions don't match this product. A bottom-up estimate (sections 1–4) is far more defensible.

### Gaps
- No market report specifically for "weather verification" or "forensic weather for claims" was found.

## 6. Demand signals (search trends, app reviews, cheap-report volume)

### Takeaway
The demand signals are mostly indirect: millions of wind/hail claims a year, a 30% wrong-date-of-loss rate, and rising hail loss costs. No public data shows how many cheap ($3–$50) reports StormRecord or its peers sell. App review counts are small, which suggests consumer self-serve report buying is still a niche.

### Cited Findings
- The Hail Recon iOS app has about 85 reviews. — [AppFollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/hail-recon/602877045?country=us)
- StormRecord's iOS listing shows $49.99 per report, versus $9.99 on its website. — `competitors.md`, citing [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stormrecord/id6758730551)
- Comparison and affiliate content (HailMate, roofingsoftwareguide) ranks for "[vendor] pricing" queries, which shows active buyer research in the contractor tier. — `competitors.md`
- Hail claims and hail loss costs are trending up (2023 a 5-year high, H1 2024 up 8%, 2024 loss cost 19% above average). — [Verisk H1 2024](https://www.verisk.com/4aba4b/siteassets/media/campaigns/gated/claims/claimsearch-trends-report-h1-2024.pdf); [LexisNexis](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/35641779/lexisnexis-u-s-home-insurance-trends-report-highlights-rising-peril-severity-as-catastrophic-claims-hit-seven-year-high)

### Inferences — overall tiered market estimate (US, 2024–2026, all Low confidence unless noted)

| Tier | Example players | Price point | Est. annual US spend | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cheap instant reports | StormRecord, WeatherGuidance, HailPoint, StormAuditor, StormerSite, HailTrace $40, AnythingWeather $40 | $3–$50 | **~$2–10M** | No volume data. Small vendors with no disclosed traction. Guess bracket. |
| Contractor hail-map subscriptions | HailTrace, IHM/Hail Recon, HailStrike, HailWatch | $149–$2,500/yr | **~$15–40M** | Aggregator revenue estimates of $1–5M each. Much of it is lead-gen, not verification. |
| Carrier enterprise tools | Verisk Benchmark, ISO ClaimSearch weather, Cotality Weather Verify | Unpublished, bundled | **~$20–60M** | About 0.7–2M reports/yr × $5–30, or bundled subscriptions. Not broken out by Verisk. |
| Forensic meteorology | CCMs, J.S. Held, CompuWeather, AccuWeather/DTN forensics | $1K–$25K+/case; $300–550/hr | **~$30–85M** | 100–200 practitioners × hours × rate. |
| NCEI certified records | NOAA NCEI, regional climate centers | ~$180/order | **~$1–5M** | No published volume. Guess bracket. |
| **Total** | | | **~$70–200M/yr** | Order of magnitude only. |

- The whole US paid weather-evidence market is probably **on the order of $100M/yr** (Low). It is dominated by carrier and forensic spend that a $9.99 self-serve report does not reach.
- The addressable slice for a $9.99 report is the non-carrier buyer (homeowner, public adjuster, small contractor, attorney's paralegal) per claim. Illustration: 2–3M wind/hail claims/yr; if 1–5% buy a $9.99 report, that is 20K–150K reports, or **about $0.2–1.5M/yr** of revenue potential. That is a hypothetical, not data.

### Gaps
- Google Trends data for "hail report" and "hail map" could not be retrieved in this session (no Trends API or page access). The keyword research in `keywords_serp.md` may help.
- No sales numbers for any cheap-report seller (StormRecord, WeatherGuidance, StormAuditor). This is the single most important missing number for the $9.99 thesis, and only test sales will answer it.
