# US Weather-Related Insurance Claim Volumes (demand base for a $9.99 per-address historical weather report)

Research notes, October 2026. US only. Every figure has its year and source. **[EST]** marks my own estimates and arithmetic. Research limits: direct page fetches failed (DNS/proxy blocks) for iii.org, verisk.com, statefarm.com, insurancejournal.com and spc.noaa.gov, so many figures come from search-result extracts of those pages or from trade-press coverage of them. Each one is flagged where that applies.

## 1. Homeowners claims overall: count, frequency, and weather share

### Takeaway
US homeowners lines produce roughly **5–9 million claims a year**, depending on the source and the year: Verisk ClaimSearch counted 6.5M in 2024 and 5.27M in 2025, and NAIC-based Weiss data shows 8.8M closed in 2023. About 5.3–5.8 of every 100 insured homes file a claim each year. Wind/hail is the single largest cause by count: about 1 in 35 insured homes has a wind/hail claim each year.

### Cited Findings
- Verisk ClaimSearch counted **5.27 million homeowners claims in 2025**, down 19% from **~6.5 million in 2024** (peak year, hurricane-driven). It was the lowest total in five years. Verisk attributes the drop mainly to a quiet hurricane season. — [Claims Journal, Apr 14 2026](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/04/14/336889.htm); [Verisk news release PDF](https://s29.q4cdn.com/767340216/files/doc_news/Insurance-Risk-Paradox-Claims-Volume-Declined-but-Risk-is-More-Elevated-and-Concentrated-New-Verisk-Report-Finds-2026.pdf); [Verisk blog](https://www.verisk.com/blog/what-2025-claims-data-is-really-telling-you/)
- Verisk: property claims volume **rose 36% in 2024**, driven mostly by hurricane catastrophe claims. — [Claims Journal, Mar 13 2025](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2025/03/13/329461.htm); [Triple-I blog](https://insuranceindustryblog.iii.org/claims-volume-up-36in-2024-climate-costs-litigation-drive-trend/)
- NAIC filings analysed by Weiss Ratings: **8.8 million homeowners claims closed in 2023** across all reporting companies. (This is a different universe from ClaimSearch and includes farmowners.) — [Weiss Ratings](https://weissratings.com/en/weiss-ratings-daily/homeowners-beware-big-insurers-deny-half-of-damage-claims); [National Mortgage News](https://www.nationalmortgagenews.com/news/13-largest-homeowners-insurers-denied-nearly-half-of-claims-last-year)
- Triple-I / ISO: homeowners claim frequency was **5.45 claims per 100 house-years in 2022**, against a **5.79 average for 2018–2022**. — [Triple-I Facts + Statistics: Homeowners and renters](https://iii.org/fact-statistic/facts-statistics-homeowners-and-renters-insurance) (via search extract)
- Triple-I / ISO: wind and hail is the largest share of claims. **About 2.8% of insured homes** (≈1 in 35) have a wind/hail claim each year (2018–2022 average). — [Triple-I](https://iii.org/fact-statistic/facts-statistics-homeowners-and-renters-insurance)
- 2023 frequency of 5.3 per 100 and wind/hail ≈1 in 36 homes: **unverified**. This comes from a secondary blog citing ISO. — [PNW Residences blog](https://www.pnwresidences.com/blog/home-insurance-claims-statistics-2026/)
- LexisNexis: wind peril claim frequency **rose 14.8% from 2022 to 2023** (different methodology from ISO). — [LexisNexis US Home Trends Report 2024](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/-/media/files/insurance/home%20trends%20report/2024%20report/us_home_trends_report_2024.pdf)
- Verisk: **non-catastrophe wind/hail made up 25% of residential roof claims in 2024**, up from 17% in 2022. Residential roof claims totalled about **$31B in 2024**. — [Carrier Management / Verisk](https://www.carriermanagement.com/brand-spotlight/verisk/verisk-roof-claims-topped-30b-in-2024-driven-by-wind-and-hail-losses/)
- Verisk property estimating: wind and hail were **57% of all claim assignments in Q2 2024**, and went from 47% (Q1 2025) to **63% (Q2 2025)**. — [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/property/verisk-highlights-rising-claims-and-roofing-costs-in-q2-2024-property-report-507330.aspx); [Verisk Q2 2025 Quarterly Property Report](https://www.verisk.com/4a05b5/siteassets/media/downloads/property-estimating/verisk-2025-q3-quarterly-property-report.pdf)

### Inferences
- **[EST]** With about 90M+ insured homes at ~2.8% wind/hail frequency, the US has about **2.0–2.8 million homeowners wind/hail claims a year**. The insured-home count is my assumption, not sourced here; the 2.8% rate is from Triple-I. That is roughly 35–45% of all homeowners claims, which fits Verisk's 47–63% quarterly shares of wind/hail in spring/summer assignments.
- **[EST]** Adding hurricane claims (large in 2022 and 2024, small in 2025), water/freeze from winter storms, and lightning puts **weather-caused homeowners claims at about 2.5–4 million a year**. Most of the volatility comes from hurricanes.

### Gaps
- No direct-fetch confirmation of the Triple-I table for 2023–2024, or of a full peril-by-count breakdown. iii.org fetch failed.
- J.D. Power's property claims studies report satisfaction, not claim counts. No count data was found.

## 2. Hail claims per year (homeowners + auto + commercial)

### Takeaway
Hail alone generates roughly **0.8–1.5 million US insurance claims a year** across lines, and big years run 50%+ above quiet ones. Verisk said 2023 set the all-time high for hail claims and that H1 2024 ran about 8% above H1 2023. A 2025 breakdown reported in trade coverage is about 835K homeowners, 329K personal auto, 36K commercial property, and 11K commercial auto hail claims, roughly **1.21M total** (attribution not fully verified).

### Cited Findings
- NICB (ISO ClaimSearch data): **~2.77 million hail claims over 2017–2019**, falling from about **1.14M (2017)** to about **785K (2019)**. — [NICB hail report](https://nicb.org/media/1923/download); [NICB Top 5 states](https://nicb.org/node/20125)
- NICB historical counts: **720,473 (2013), 824,325 (2014), 572,182 (2015)**. — [NICB Hail Claims Report 2013–2015 via Carrier Management](https://carriermanagement.com/research/?p=10737); [NICB](https://nicb.org/media/1547/download)
- Verisk ClaimSearch: hail claim volume hit an **all-time high in 2023**. 2023 had **~50% more hail claims than any of the prior four years**, and **H1 2024 was up ~8% vs H1 2023**. — [Verisk ClaimSearch Trends H1 2024](https://www.verisk.com/4aba4b/siteassets/media/campaigns/gated/claims/claimsearch-trends-report-h1-2024.pdf); [Verisk ClaimSearch Trends 2023 Year-End](https://www.verisk.com/498a68/siteassets/iso-claimsearch/downloads/claimsearch-trends-2023-year-end-report.pdf)
- 2025 hail claims by line: **835,058 homeowners; 328,606 personal auto; 35,812 commercial property; 11,064 commercial auto** (sum ≈1.21M). **Attribution caveat:** these appeared in a search-engine synthesis of Verisk 2025 coverage ([Claims Journal](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/04/14/336889.htm), [Leader's Edge "Hail Is Falling, Losses Aren't"](https://www.leadersedge.com/p-c/hail-is-falling-losses-arent)). I could not fetch the page to confirm them, and an exact-phrase search for "835,058" returned nothing. Treat them as plausible but unverified.
- State Farm (largest US home/auto insurer) hail payouts: **~$5B in 2024** (top 10 states >80%, $4.0B) and **>$5.6B in 2025**. — [State Farm newsroom, 2025 hail](https://newsroom.statefarm.com/state-farm-paid-over-56-billion-in-hail-claims-in-2025/); [Carrier Management](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2026/04/21/287038.htm)
- A Feb 2024 article splits State Farm hail payouts as ~$2.9B home and ~$0.6B auto (year unclear, probably 2023). — [PropertyCasualty360](https://propertycasualty360.com/2024/02/26/these-states-see-the-most-hail-related-claims)
- An aggregator says "State Farm paid 27,300 hail claims in 2023". **This looks implausibly low** for State Farm's scale and is probably a state-level or misread figure. Do not use it. — [Rethinking65](https://rethinking65.com/?p=15216)

### Inferences
- **[EST]** Range for planning: about **0.8M hail claims (quiet year) to 1.5M+ (big year such as 2023)** across all lines. Homeowners are about 65–70% and personal auto about 25–30%.

### Gaps
- No verified hail claim count for 2023 or 2024 itself. The Verisk year-end PDFs are gated, and the fetch failed.

## 3. Lightning claims per year

### Takeaway
Lightning produces about **55K–80K homeowners claims a year**: 70,787 in 2023, 55,537 in 2024, and 61,986 in 2025. Count is falling over time while severity rises (avg $26,616 in 2025).

### Cited Findings
- Triple-I / State Farm: lightning homeowners claims were **70,787 (2023), 55,537 (2024, the lowest since before 2017), and 61,986 (2025, +11.6%)**. Payouts were **$1.24B (2023), $1.04B (2024), and $1.65B (2025)**. The 2025 average claim was **$26,616, up 42.8%**. — [Business Wire / Triple-I, Jun 18 2026](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260618730348/en/Triple-I-Lightning-Caused-%241.65-Billion-in-US-Homeowners-Claim-Payouts-in-2025-Average-Cost-Per-Claim-Surges-Nearly-43); [Reinsurance News (2024)](https://www.reinsurancene.ws/us-lightning-related-insurance-claims-down-16-5-in-2024-triple-i/); [Insurance Business (2024)](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/property/us-insurers-pay-1-04-billion-in-lightningrelated-homeowners-insurance-claims-539881.aspx)
- Top states by lightning claim count, 2025: **Florida 5,167, California 4,211, Texas 4,188**. Texas has the highest average cost ($60,382 in 2025). — [InsuranceNewsNet / Triple-I](https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/triple-i-lightning-caused-1-65-billion-in-us-homeowners-claim-payouts-in-2025-average-cost-per-claim-surges-nearly-43)
- State Farm: ground surges cause **nearly half** of lightning claims. — [Agency Checklists](https://agencychecklists.com/2026/06/20/lightning-insurance-claims-severity-2025-82447/)

### Inferences
- Lightning claims often turn on whether a strike occurred near the address on that date. That makes them well suited to a per-address strike-proximity report. Average severity above $26K also means a $9.99 report is negligible relative to the stakes.

### Gaps
- Lightning claims on commercial or auto policies were not found.

## 4. Auto comprehensive hail claims

### Takeaway
Hail is about **10–12% of auto comprehensive claims**. Annual auto hail claims are roughly **0.3M in a normal year and higher in big years**, with spikes such as Q3 2023 alone at about 130K.

### Cited Findings
- CCC: hail was **11.8% of all comprehensive claims in 2023**, up from 9% in 2020. Hail claims cost **21.7% more** than the average comprehensive claim, and **80%** included paintless dent repair. — [CCC Crash Course Q2 2024](https://cccis.com/news-and-insights/posts/ccc-crash-course-report-highlights-the-growing-impact-of-severe-weather-events-on-auto-insurance-and-collision-repair-industries); [Insurance Journal](https://amp.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2024/07/01/781819.htm)
- Verisk ClaimSearch: auto hail claims **spiked to ~0.13M in Q3 2023, +200% vs Q3 2022**. — [Verisk ClaimSearch Trends Q3 2023](https://www.verisk.com/4aae3b/siteassets/media/campaigns/gated/claims/claimsearch-trends-q3-2023.pdf)
- 2025 personal auto hail claims: **328,606** (unverified attribution; see §2). Total personal auto claims were about **31.6M in 2025**, against a 2022 peak of 34.4M. — [Claims Journal](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/04/14/336889.htm)

### Inferences
- **[EST]** Auto hail is a large count but a weak fit for the product. These claims are low severity, comprehensive coverage is rarely disputed on date, and PDR shops and insurers mostly use catastrophe event codes. They are a secondary audience at best.

### Gaps
- No CCC or Verisk absolute auto hail count for full-year 2023 or 2024 was found.

## 5. Commercial property weather claims

### Takeaway
Commercial property is a much smaller count pool: about **0.7–0.9M total claims a year**, with an estimated ~36K hail claims in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Verisk: commercial property claims **fell to ~710,000 in 2025, from ~910,000 in 2023**. — [Claims Journal](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/04/14/336889.htm); [Carrier Management](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2026/04/14/286721.htm)
- Commercial property hail claims in 2025: **35,812**, plus commercial auto hail claims of **11,064** (unverified attribution; see §2).

### Gaps
- No commercial wind or lightning counts were found.

## 6. Disputed, denied, delayed, or wrong-date-of-loss claims

### Takeaway
A large minority of weather claims involve a contested fact this report could address. Verisk says **30–40% of hail claims have an incorrect or inconsistent date of loss**, and **37–47% of homeowners claims closed without payment in 2023**. Formal disputes (appraisal, litigation) are a few percent of claims nationally, but they are concentrated in FL, TX, OK, and CO.

### Cited Findings
- Verisk (vendor marketing for its Benchmark weather-verification product): **~40% of hail claims have an inconsistent date of loss** (XactAnalysis data), and **~1%** have a corrected date of loss **before policy inception**. Verisk sells per-address weather reports for this purpose (Benchmark). — [Verisk Benchmark](https://www.verisk.com/insurance/products/benchmark/); [Verisk severe weather claims page](https://www.verisk.com/insurance/campaigns/data-powered-claims-solutions-for-severe-weather-and-catastrophe-response/)
- Earlier figure: **~30% of hail claims have an incorrect date of loss** (Verisk Climate research, 2015 press release). — [GlobeNewswire, 2015](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2015/03/31/720595/0/en/xactware-and-verisk-climate-make-it-easier-to-validate-weather-related-claims.html)
- Weiss Ratings (NAIC data): in 2023, **37.4% of 8.8M homeowners claims closed without payment**, against 24.9% in an earlier year. The 13 largest insurers denied **47.5% of 3.9M**, ranging up to 70.5% (Farm Bureau P&C). — [Weiss Ratings](https://weissratings.com/en/weiss-ratings-daily/homeowners-beware-big-insurers-deny-half-of-damage-claims)
- Secondary sources cite **42.1% closed without payment nationally for 2024**. This conflicts with the 37.4% (2023) Weiss baseline; the primary source was not verified. — [Dick Law Firm blog](https://www.dicklawfirm.com/blog/2026/april/have-more-home-insurance-claims-been-denied-sinc/)
- Texas: about **47% of home claims closed without payment in 2024**, against about 35% in 2016 (Houston Chronicle analysis, repeated by secondary sources). — [Carroll County News press release](https://www.mycarrollcountynews.com/online_features/press_releases/article_70deac98-f950-5481-a69d-4bd3496d2029.html)
- Note: "closed without payment" includes claims below the deductible, which is common for wind/hail with percentage deductibles. It is **not** the same as a disputed claim.
- Florida OIR: Florida had **~15% of US homeowners claims but ~71% of homeowners claims litigation in 2022**. Its share of national homeowners lawsuits fell from **79% (2020) to 41% (2025)** after the 2022–2023 reforms. Litigation rates remain higher for wind and hail claims. — [Claims Journal / FL OIR Jan 2024](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2024/01/16/321502.htm); [Insurance Business, 2026](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/property/florida-homeowners-insurance-lawsuit-share-drops-to-41-after-reform-push-583458.aspx); [FL OIR litigation report](https://www.floir.gov/docs-sf/default-source/property-and-casualty/other-property-casualty-reports/january-2024-pclr.pdf)
- Texas: appraisal is used on about **2% of claims**, and pre-appraisal lawsuits fell from 3.5% (2021) to 0.4% (2023). This is a law-firm reading of TDI data. Texas SB 458 requires an appraisal clause in residential and personal auto policies issued or renewed on or after **Jan 1 2026**. — [Donegan Law blog](https://donegan.com/blog/texas-insurance-appraisal-rules-sb-458-home-auto-claims/); [TDI weather-related claims report](https://www.tdi.texas.gov/reports/documents/weatherrelatedpropertyclaims.pdf)
- Oklahoma: **600+ pending hail/wind claim-handling lawsuits** (reported). — [Beinsure](https://beinsure.com/news/hail-claims-disputes-rise-as-u-s-home-insurance-pressure-builds/)

### Inferences
- **[EST] Addressable "contested-fact" pool:** about 1M+ homeowners hail claims a year × 30–40% with date-of-loss inconsistency gives roughly **300K–500K hail claims a year where the date or occurrence of weather at the address is in question**. Add wind and lightning claims with similar issues, and the pool is plausibly **0.4–0.8M claims a year**. These are the strongest buyers, whether policyholders, public adjusters, contractors, or small independent adjusters. Note that the main source for the 30–40% figure is Verisk, which sells a competing product.
- **[EST]** Formal disputes (appraisal plus litigation) run in the low single-digit percent of weather claims nationally, perhaps **tens of thousands a year**, heavily concentrated in TX, FL, OK and CO.

### Gaps
- No neutral (non-vendor) study of the date-of-loss error rate was found.
- No national wind/hail-specific denial rate was found.

## 7. Geographic concentration and year-to-year volatility

### Takeaway
Hail claims are heavily concentrated: **Texas alone is often 20–25%+ of national hail claims**, and the top 10 states (TX, IL, MO, CO, NE, OK, KS, MN, IA, plus a rotating 10th) take about 80% of State Farm hail dollars. Annual hail claim counts swing by **±30–50%** year to year.

### Cited Findings
- State Farm top 10 hail states by payout, 2024: **TX $1.1B, IL $638M** (State Farm Illinois release says $899M, a conflict), **MO $580M, CO $521M, NE $342M, OK $338M, KS $170M, MN $124M, IA $109M, SC $109M**. The top 10 is >80% of the ~$5B national total. — [Carrier Management / State Farm](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2026/04/21/287038.htm); [State Farm Illinois release](https://newsroom.statefarm.com/state-farm-data-shows-illinois-severe-weather-trend-is-accelerating/)
- 2025: Texas led with **$1.4B**, followed by MO, IL, WI and OK. WI, KY, AR and IN were new to the top 10. — [State Farm newsroom](https://newsroom.statefarm.com/state-farm-paid-over-56-billion-in-hail-claims-in-2025/)
- NICB 2017–2019: Texas had **637,977 hail claims** (~23% of the 2.77M national total); 2016–2018 Texas had 811,381. — [NICB](https://nicb.org/node/20125); [FenderBender](https://www.fenderbender.com/articles/14953-texas-leads-nation-in-claims-for-hail-damage)
- Volatility examples: NICB counts fell from 1.14M (2017) to 785K (2019), a −31% change. 2014 to 2015 fell from 824K to 572K (−31%). Verisk says 2023 was **~50% above any of the prior four years**. Homeowners claims overall fell **−19%** from 2024 to 2025. — sources as in §1–2.
- Texas recorded **902 major hail events in 2025**, the most of any state. — [search extract, Texas appraisal coverage](https://www.hmf-law.com/news/texas-hail-claim-appraisal-law-sb-458/)

### Inferences
- A single-region v1 is well supported by this distribution. **Texas (Dallas–Fort Worth / North Texas)** or **Colorado Front Range** each covers a large share of national hail claims. Texas also has the high closed-without-payment rate (~47%) and the new SB 458 appraisal regime, both of which create demand for documented weather evidence.

### Gaps
- No state-level claim **counts** (rather than dollars) were found for 2023–2025.

## 8. Event volume proxy: SPC storm reports and NOAA Storm Events

### Takeaway
NWS/SPC log roughly **~5,000–7,000 hail reports a year** (~7,000 in 2023) and more for damaging wind. NOAA's Storm Events Database adds about **60,000–80,000 event records a year** across all types. That is enough weather events to back roughly a million hail claims.

### Cited Findings
- NOAA annual summary: about **7,000 hail reports in 2023** (as reported). SPC logged **1,077 very-large-hail reports through Dec 22 2023**, more than 150% above the typical ~200 baseball-size-or-larger reports. 2023 and 2024 both set records for annual 2-inch-plus hail reports. — [The Weather Network](https://www.theweathernetwork.com/en/news/weather/severe/2023-was-the-year-of-massive-hail-across-north-america-europe-canada-united-states-italy); [AccuWeather](https://www.accuweather.com/en/severe-weather/is-climate-change-making-hailstones-larger/1652329)
- Munich Re lists **10,006 severe hail events** reported by NOAA in 2023. The label is ambiguous and conflicts with ~7,000. — [Munich Re hail facts](https://www.munichre.com/en/insights/natural-disaster-and-climate-change/hail-facts.html)
- CoreLogic: 2-inch-plus hail fell on **141 days in 2023**, the most in two decades. — [Insurance Journal, Feb 2024](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2024/02/22/761760.htm)
- SPC 2023: **1,269 preliminary confirmed tornadoes** (12th most since 1950) and **1,400 severe reports on Aug 7 2023** alone. — [SPC Year in Review](https://spc.noaa.gov/media/year-in-review)
- NOAA Storm Events Database: about **60K–80K records a year**, ~75K in the 2023 edition (third-party analysis). All 48 event types have been recorded since 1996. — [ai-analytics.org](https://ai-analytics.org/writing/noaa-storm-events-database/); [NCEI Storm Events](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/)
- The SPC publishes annual hail, wind and tornado CSVs (2024 files updated Apr 2026) at spc.noaa.gov/wcm. — [SPC WCM page](https://www.spc.noaa.gov/wcm/)
- AccuWeather caveat: more spotters inflate report counts over time. — [AccuWeather](https://www.accuweather.com/en/severe-weather/is-climate-change-making-hailstones-larger/1652329)

### Inferences
- **[EST]** About 1M hail claims spread over about 5–7K hail reports gives on the order of **~150–200 claims per hail report**. Many claims sit miles from the nearest point report, which is the "distance-and-bearing proximity" gap the product closes.

### Gaps
- Exact SPC annual hail and wind report counts for 2021–2025 were not retrieved; the proxy blocked spc.noaa.gov. They can be computed later by counting rows in the SPC `YYYY_hail.csv` and `YYYY_wind.csv` files from an unblocked network.

## Summary demand funnel (all [EST], for the report writer)

| Layer | Annual US claims (range) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| All homeowners claims | 5.3M–8.8M | Verisk 2025/2024; NAIC/Weiss 2023 |
| Homeowners weather claims (wind, hail, lightning, hurricane, winter) | ~2.5M–4M | 2.8% wind/hail frequency + hurricane/lightning |
| Hail claims, all lines | ~0.8M–1.5M | NICB 2017–19; Verisk 2023 record; 2025 ≈1.21M (unverified) |
| Lightning homeowners claims | 55K–71K | Triple-I 2023–2025 |
| Hail claims with date-of-loss inconsistency | ~300K–500K | 30–40% (Verisk, vendor claim) |
| Formally disputed (appraisal/litigation) weather claims | tens of thousands | FL OIR, TX TDI-derived, OK reports |

**[EST]** Even 1% uptake of the ~0.4–0.8M contested-fact pool at $9.99 is about 4K–8K reports, or $40K–$80K a year. This marks a ceiling on the claim-count side only. It does not estimate willingness to pay or competition (Verisk Benchmark, StormRecord, HailTrace and others).
